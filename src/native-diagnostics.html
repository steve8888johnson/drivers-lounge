Parked field-test sequence

  1. Open Navigation or Permitted Loads and start only a reviewed test route while safely parked.
  2. Lock the screen for two minutes, reopen the app, then switch to another app for two minutes.
  3. Turn airplane mode on briefly, restore service, and confirm the route remains blocked or recovers visibly rather than silently changing.
  4. Stop guidance, return here, and download the report. Any moving test must be observed by a passenger.
