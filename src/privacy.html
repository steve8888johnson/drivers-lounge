PRIVACY

Your data stays under your control.

Effective August 31, 2026. This policy describes information Drivers Lounge may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

Account details, driver or business profile information, optional mobile number, communication preferences, community posts, carrier reviews, road reports, marketplace content, support messages and other information you choose to submit.

Location information

Location may be used for navigation, nearby road intelligence and driver reports when you permit access. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings. Drivers Lounge should not require continuous location access for features that do not need it.

Advertising and Saved Offers

Drivers Lounge may show paid sponsored offers and separately labeled public offers found for drivers. We may record limited interaction events such as impressions, audio plays and saves to operate and measure the feature. Saving an offer in the app does not require email or text marketing consent.

Email and text delivery

If you explicitly enable saved-offer delivery, Drivers Lounge may use your account email or optional mobile number to send an offer you chose to save. You can turn email or text delivery off from Account settings. We do not treat a one-tap in-app save by itself as consent to unrelated marketing messages.

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts, show relevant driver tools, process community and carrier-review content, save requested offers, improve safety-related features, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and maintain the service.

Carrier reviews and community content

Published reviews and posts may be visible to other users. Drivers should avoid posting private personal information, confidential company information, threats or unsupported allegations about identifiable individuals. Reporting tools may preserve content needed for moderation review.

Service providers

Drivers Lounge may rely on infrastructure, authentication, mapping, analytics, communications and other service providers to operate the app. These providers may process limited data needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy obligations.

Retention

Information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and service operation. Public content may be retained in de-identified or moderated form where appropriate.

Account deletion

You can request deletion from the web or from the Account page. A deletion request begins a review process so identity, safety, fraud-prevention and legal-retention obligations can be handled before account data is removed or de-identified.

Your choices

You may manage location permission, communication preferences, profile information, sign out, request support, report content and request account deletion.
