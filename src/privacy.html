PRIVACY

Your data stays under your control.

Effective August 31, 2026. This policy describes information Drivers Lounge may collect, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

Account details, driver or business profile information, community posts, carrier reviews, road reports, marketplace content, support messages and other information you choose to submit.

Location information

Location may be used for navigation, nearby road intelligence and driver reports when you permit access. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device or browser settings. Drivers Lounge should not require continuous location access for features that do not need it.

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts, show relevant driver tools, process community and carrier-review content, improve safety-related features, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and maintain the service.

Carrier reviews and community content

Published reviews and posts may be visible to other users. Drivers should avoid posting private personal information, confidential company information, threats or unsupported allegations about identifiable individuals.

Service providers

Drivers Lounge may rely on infrastructure, authentication, mapping, analytics, communications and other service providers to operate the app. These providers may process limited data needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy obligations.

Retention

Information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and service operation. Public content may be retained in de-identified or moderated form where appropriate.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion from the Account page. A deletion request begins a review process so identity, safety, fraud-prevention and legal-retention obligations can be handled before account data is removed or de-identified.

Open account controls

Your choices

You may manage location permission, edit profile information, sign out, request support, report content and request account deletion. Additional privacy controls will be added as features requiring them are introduced.

Important: Drivers Lounge is being prepared for public release. This policy should be reviewed with qualified counsel before final commercial launch, especially after advertising, payments, analytics, background location or other third-party integrations are added.
