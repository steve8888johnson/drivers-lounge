How it works

Start the journal from Navigation and keep that page open. Drivers Lounge saves your GPS path and creates an estimated crossing when your device comes within 120 meters of a mapped ALPR camera.

These entries do not prove that a camera captured your plate and do not show police searches. Camera locations can be missing, moved or incorrect. Browser location recording may pause when the screen locks or the app is in the background.
