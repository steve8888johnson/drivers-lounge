# Tomorrow's permit scan and parked route review

Prepared October 14, 2026 for the expected October 15 Draper, Utah–Brunswick, Georgia permit packet. This is an intake procedure, not approval of an actual route. The actual permits and measured loaded totals are not yet available.

1. Keep the full original packet for every issuing state: identification/vehicle page, every route page, restrictions/conditions and amendments. A readable QR alone may not contain the permit number, dates, load limits or conditions. Do not assume the states from a normal car route.
2. Enter measured loaded height, total width including protrusions, length, gross weight and axle information. The estimate of one foot over normal width is not a measured total. Compare the actual vehicle/load against every permit.
3. Add all pages of **one state permit per selection**. The wallet keeps originals locally even when a scan fails. JPG/PNG/WebP photos can be scanned; PDFs can be saved, but their QR currently needs a close photo or copied QR contents. Keep the complete page as well as the close-up.
4. Read a saved QR and inspect its import option. Unknown/protected/provider formats remain pending for manual review or a supported adapter. Do not send credentials or assume a generic navigation link is an issued route. Only the two previously observed public endpoint formats have specific adapters.
5. In **Review imported changes**, compare the complete saved/proposed route text and conditions. Inspect changed identity/dates/borders, maneuver counts and retained fields. Retained conditions are not independently verified by a new QR. Cancel keeps saved details. Apply saves an unconfirmed draft, resets complete-page and review confirmations, and removes obsolete geometry if route text/entry/exit changed without replacement geometry.
6. Confirm state, permit number, effective dates, local time zone, exact origin/exit, loaded limits and every original condition. Do not infer daylight/holiday/escort rules from a clock field. The text parser recognizes only supported explicit issuer headings; a destination mentioning a state is not an issuer.
7. Add the next actual state permit and inspect every border connection. An uncovered state before the first record or after the last record now holds review, as do missing intermediate coverage, competing continuations, mismatched roads or coordinate gaps. These checks do not independently establish the real pickup, delivery, carriageway or entrance. Never erase a real border state just to make the check pass.
8. Review lawful supplied geometry and each maneuver against the originals. Geometry is not generated to bridge gaps. Text too large for supported fields is rejected whole rather than silently shortened. A correct QR decode or matching instruction does not prove a road is open or suitable.
9. Use the parked walkthrough and spoken review after the current original/geometry checks pass. Compare each state transition, movement window, escort instruction and destination access. Driving guidance remains disabled pending authoritative/field evidence.
10. Export the trip **with originals** as a backup. The current computer wallet does not automatically appear on the iPhone. Import that packet in Safari, review it again, and complete the parked phone test at https://driverslounge.app/permit-phone-check. A saved phone test cannot unlock driving guidance.

## Stop and investigate

Wrong state or permit number; truncated/cropped/missing pages; amendments inconsistent with an earlier QR; limits inconsistent with the measured load; expired or ambiguous dates/windows; unverified entrances/road directions; missing or conflicting state connections; unsupported QR data; missing originals after device transfer. Preserve the evidence and obtain the corrected/missing information. Never generate an unapproved detour.

## Engineering evidence

The October14 import-review pass adds whole-field rejection, expected-state validation for supported public responses, first/last-state coverage holds, full before/after import comparison and stale-result protection. Automated cases and synthetic local browser checks establish those software behaviors only. No actual new-load permit, road route, iPhone camera or field navigation has been validated by this pass.
