# Joined ramp comparison for parked rehearsal

The previous inspection showed one inventory record at a time, so a short slip ending inside a longer connector could not be viewed as one candidate while rehearsing the two printed instructions. The optional joined comparison now displays those original pieces together and lets Ramp entry / Merge focus on its start / end. Read remains a separate tap and reads the literal issuer row, with an explicit candidate-endpoint notice.

This does not resolve the SH31 source mismatch or approve its exact maneuver coordinates. The issuer still supplies no coordinate-to-instruction binding. Dated aerial/plan support remains historical evidence, as documented in PERMIT-SH31-AERIAL-EVIDENCE-20261016.md. No new current-field or authoritative maneuver evidence was established this turn.

## Geometry and lifecycle rules

Candidates require an adjacent literal Take-road-Ramp / Merge-onto-road pair; selected single-part connector geometry; one exactly shared original vertex; same inventory date; both comparison endpoints within the query bounds; and geometric start-to-end compass order matching the printed compass. All matching candidates are offered without a default. Compass agreement is not traffic-direction permission. Source points may be traversed in reverse for comparison, never silently called an allowed movement.

No interpolation, near-point snapping, synthetic connections or gap bridging. Shared interior points of the second record may be used to retain just its original relevant portion. Repeated/overlapping vertices, multipart shapes, missing/near-only joins, clipped endpoints, different dates, unsupported long/sparse chains and excess candidates are excluded. Candidate paths are detached copies. Neither steps nor approvals are generated; approval and travel-direction flags remain false.

The complete joined shape is compared to the unchanged issuer line, so selecting a short initial record cannot hide a larger discrepancy on the continuation. Switching the pair stops prior speech and focuses the corresponding candidate endpoint without autoplay or a new query. Ordinary row/road changes, hide/close, missing evidence and stale responses clear the selection. No candidate is saved as a route or GPS cue.
